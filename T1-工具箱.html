数据表达 — 数量的多种说法

同一篇文章里换2-3种说法，不要所有地方都用 the number of

说法用于例句
the figure for最通用The figure for mobile calls rose to 45 billion.
the number of可数名词The number of students increased.
the amount of不可数名词The amount of money spent on...
the proportion of比例/占比The proportion of car users was highest.
the percentage of百分比The percentage of people cycling grew.
the level of水平/程度The level of pollution decreased.
the rate of比率/速度The rate of unemployment fell.
