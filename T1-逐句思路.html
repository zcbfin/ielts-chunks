Universal Framework
1 — 2 — 5 — 5
Intro 1句改写题目 · Overview 2句宏观结论（不写数字）· Details 1 ~5句第一组数据 · Details 2 ~5句第二组数据

每段的"功能"是什么

Intro = 1 句 · 改写题目

换动词(shows→compares)、换时间(between→from...to)、删below。
公式：The [图型] + [动词] + [主题] + in + [地点] + [时间]

Overview = 2 句 · 整张图最大的发现

考官先看这里！绝不写具体数字。句1=最高/整体趋势。句2=最低/例外/另一张图。
开头：It is clear that... 第二句：By contrast / However / While...

Details 1 = ~5 句 · 第一组数据

选3-5个关键数字。按分组（前半时间/类别A/图1）写。句型不要重复：S+V锚点 → saw换主语 → while对比 → 补充趋势。

Details 2 = ~5 句 · 第二组数据

选另外3-5个数字（不同时间/类别/图2）。过渡词：Turning to... / With regard to... / Subsequently...

Essay 1 · 线图

UK 三类电话 1995-2002 · Band 9 · 197 words

1

Bar Chart（有时间=当线图写）· 分组：时间前半 vs 后半

策略A 最高vs最低
1
改写题目
shows→compares 加UK 换时间表达
The bar chart compares the amount of time spent by people in the UK on three different types of phone call between 1995 and 2002.
2
总览句1：最高
哪个最多？local fixed。
不写数字。
It is clear that calls made via local, fixed lines were the most popular type, in terms of overall usage, throughout the period shown.
3
总览句2：最低+例外
mobile最低但增幅最大。
saw句型。
The lowest figures on the chart are for mobile calls, but this category also saw the most dramatic increase in user minutes.
4
D1锚点：1995初始数据
S+V主动。local=70bn
national=35bn mobile=4bn
In 1995, people in the UK used fixed lines for a total of just over 70 billion minutes for local calls, and about half of that amount for national or international calls. By contrast, mobile phones were only used for around 4 billion minutes.
5
D1趋势过渡
接下来四年三类都涨。
时间过渡句。
Over the following four years, the figures for all three types of phone call increased steadily.
6
D2峰值+趋势分化
1999年local peak at 90bn
然后跌 但另两类继续涨
By 1999, local calls using landlines had reached a peak at 90 billion minutes. Subsequently, this category fell, but the rise in the other two types continued.
7
D2收束：最终数据
national=60bn mobile=45bn
while对比两条线
In 2002, national/international calls passed 60 billion, while mobiles rose to around 45 billion minutes.

1-2-5-5 分配

Intro = 1句(句1)

改写compares+UK+between...and

Overview = 2句(句2-3)

策略A。句2=最高(local) 句3=最低但增长最大(mobile)

Details 1 = 2句(句4-5)

S+V给1995年三个数据→By contrast→过渡到1999整体趋势

Details 2 = 2句(句6-7)

1999峰值+saw→Subsequently分化→while对比最终数据收束

Essay 2 · 饼图

Parkway Hotel 满意度 · 双饼 2005 vs 2010 · Band 9 · 193 words

2

Pie Charts ×2 · 分组：正面评价 vs 负面评价

策略A变体 · 类别分组
1
改写题目
双饼用 compare
同一问卷两个年份
The pie charts compare visitors' responses to a survey about customer service at the Parkway Hotel in 2005 and in 2010.
2
总览句1：趋势
满意度整体提升。
不写数字。
It is clear that overall customer satisfaction increased considerably from 2005 to 2010.
3
总览句2：对比
2005=大多数人不满
2010=大多数人满意
While most hotel guests rated customer service as satisfactory or poor in 2005, a clear majority described it as good or excellent in 2010.
4-5
D1：正面评价
Looking at positive first
excellent 5%→28%
good 14%→three times
Looking at the positive responses first, in 2005 only 5% rated customer service as excellent, but this figure rose to 28% in 2010. Furthermore, while only 14% described it as good in 2005, almost three times as many gave this rating five years later.
6-7
D2：负面评价
With regard to negative
poor 21%→12%
very poor 15%→4%
satisfactory降=正面升
With regard to negative feedback, the proportion who considered service poor fell from 21% to only 12%. Similarly, those who thought service was very poor dropped from 15% to only 4%. Finally, a fall in 'satisfactory' ratings reflects the fact that more people gave positive responses in that year.

1-2-5-5 分配

Intro = 1句(句1)

compare + in 2005 and in 2010

Overview = 2句(句2-3)

句2=整体满意度提升 句3=while对比两个饼的核心差异

Details 1 = 2句(句4-5)

正面评价（excellent+good） 两个数据点+对比+倍数

Details 2 = 2句(句6-7)

负面评价（poor+very poor+satisfactory） 三个数据点+收束关联D1

Essay 6 · 混合图

Living Alone · Bar + Pie · Band 9 · 189 words

6

Bar + Pie 混合 · England 2011 · 策略B一图一句

分组：按图分
1
改写题目（混合图）
两图各用一个动词
bar compares+pie shows
The bar chart compares figures for occupants' age and gender, and the pie chart shows data about the number of bedrooms.
2
总览句1：bar chart
女性居多数
老年组尤明显
Overall, females made up a higher proportion than males, and this is particularly noticeable in the older age categories.
3
总览句2：pie chart
双卧最普遍
We can also see that the most common number of bedrooms was two.
4
D1：bar chart
老年女性72% 76%
年轻男性65%
By contrast分性别
Women made up around 72% of occupants aged 75-84, and 76% aged 85+. By contrast, among younger adults men accounted for nearly 65% of those living alone.
5
D2：pie chart
双卧35.4%
单卧28% 三卧29.8%
四卧+不到7%
35.4% of homes had two bedrooms, while one- and three-bedroom homes accounted for 28% and 29.8%. Under 7% had four or more bedrooms.

1-2-5-5 分配

Intro = 1句(句1)

混合图公式：图A动词 + and + 图B动词

Overview = 2句(句2-3)

策略B一图一句。句2=bar chart(女性居多数) 句3=pie chart(双卧最常见)

Details 1 = 1句(句4)

全部写给bar chart。老年vs青年→性别对比

Details 2 = 1句(句5)

全部写给pie chart。while连接数据。under 7%补极端值
