2條鐵律

1. 永遠不要寫新內容。2. 1句話：重複/總結你在Introduction中的立場。
技巧：Paraphrase你的intro answer。用不同的詞說同樣的立場。

Planning 步驟（10分鐘內完成）

Step 1：Read and understand the question

  • Highlight/underline key parts of the question
  • Identify question type (Discussion/Opinion/Problem-Solution/Two-part)

Step 2：Plan your essay structure (4 paragraphs)

  • Introduction: topic + your answer
  • Body 1: first viewpoint/reason
  • Body 2: second viewpoint/reason (your view for Discussion)
  • Conclusion: paraphrase your answer

Step 3：Brainstorm ideas for main paragraphs

  • Note down any ideas you have
  • Develop ideas in detail (not just keywords, but full thoughts)
  • Choose the best 2-3 ideas per paragraph
