Task 1

小作文 · 四段万用框架 1-2-5-5

Universal Formula · 所有图型通用
1-2-5-5
Introduction = 1句 · Overview = 2句 · Details 1 = ~5句 · Details 2 = ~5句
Introduction: 1句 = 改写题目
Overview: 2句 = 主要趋势 + 最高最低（不写数字）
Details 1: ~5句 = 第一组关键数字 · 趋势 · 对比
Details 2: ~5句 = 第二组关键数字 · 趋势 · 对比
总共约150-180词 · 不写Conclusion
Step 1 · 所有图型通用

Introduction：1句话，只做改写

万用公式： The + [图型名] + [动词] + [主题是什么] + [在哪里] + [什么时间]

图型完整公式（直接套）
线图The line graph compares [主题] in [地点] between [年] and [年].
柱状图（有时间）The bar chart compares [主题] in [地点] between [年] and [年].
柱状图（无时间）The bar chart compares [主题] in [类别A, B, C...].
饼图The pie chart(s) give(s) information about [主题] in [地点/年].
表格The table shows/compares [主题] in [地点/年].
流程图The diagram illustrates [过程的完整名称].
地图The map(s) show(s) [主题] in [地点] (between [年] and [年]).
动词替换（shows → ）
shows 可以换成：compares（最常用）/ illustrates（更正式）/ gives information about（最安全）
时间改写（from X to Y → ）
from 2001 to 2010 → between 2001 and 2010 / over a period of 10 years / over the 10-year period

7种图型 · 各题型专属公式

线图 Line Graph

有时间的动态图 · 先升后降/持续上升/两线交叉

Overview公式：

万能Overview
It is clear that [整体趋势]. [例外/最显著的变化].

Details关键表达：

  • 峰值: reached a peak of [数字] in [年], then declined to [数字] by [年]
  • 增长: increased steadily from [数字] to [数字] between [年] and [年]
  • 反超: [A] overtook [B] in [年] / surpassed

柱状图 Bar Chart

分两种：有时间的=当线图写 / 无时间的=比大小

有时间→动态描述：

[对象] saw a dramatic increase from [数字] to [数字].

无时间→静态比较：

[X] was the most popular, followed by [Y] (at [%]), and [Z] respectively.

饼图 Pie Chart

占比=核心 · 从大到小排列着写

Details公式（按大小排）：

The largest segment accounted for [%], followed by [X] (at [%]), with the remaining share going to [Y] and [Z].

占比动词轮换: accounted for / constituted / made up / represented / comprised

表格 Table

数据多 = 必须精选。只选最高/最低/变化最大

策略：先横比（类别）再纵比（时间）

In terms of [类别A], [X] stood at [数字] in [年], compared to [数字] for [Y].

选数字原则：每段3-5个，不堆砌

流程图 Process / Diagram

全部用被动态！are/is + 过去分词

单步骤公式：

[材料] + is + [动词过去分词] + in/by + [器具/方式]

5种步骤合并方式：

  1. where 定语从句：...oven, where it is heated
  2. before being：...oven, before being heated
  3. after which：...first stage, after which it is heated
  4. until：...oven, until it is melted
  5. and 直连：...heated and turned into liquid

结尾段： The process consists of [N] stages. It starts from [起点] and ends in [终点].

地图 Map

全部 was + 过去分词 · 一般过去时

5组变化动词：

新增was added / constructed / built / introduced
替换was replaced by / converted into / turned into
拆除was demolished / removed / knocked down
扩建was extended / expanded / enlarged
移位was relocated / moved to

方位表达：to the north/south/east/west of · adjacent to · in the centre of · on the outskirts of ★

混合图 Mixed Charts

两张图 = 一段写一张

Intro一句话概括两图：

The [图A] compares [A主题], and the [图B] shows [B主题].

Overview两句话（一句一图）：

It is clear that [图一发现]. In terms of [图二], [图二发现].

D1写图一 · D2 Turning to 图二 · 有关联加：This pattern is reflected in the second chart.

Overview 段 · 万能公式大全

Overview 是整篇最重要的两句话。考官先看这里。不写具体数字！

策略公式例句
最高 vs 最低
60%题目用这个		 It is clear that [最高/最大]. By contrast, [最低/最小]. It is clear that local fixed-line calls were the most popular. By contrast, mobile calls recorded by far the lowest figures.
趋势 + 例外
有反转的图用		 It is clear that [整体趋势]. However, [最显著的例外/反转]. It is clear that beef was the most popular meat. However, a considerable rise can be seen in broilers, with figures eventually surpassing those for beef.
图一 + 图二
双图/混合图必用		 It is clear that [图一发现]. In terms of [图二主题], [图二发现]. It is clear that parents spent more each year. In terms of children taking part, football was the most popular.
Overview开头句 5选1
★★★★★ It is clear that + [发现]（67篇出现40+次）
★★★★ It is noticeable that + [特征]（更正式）
★★★ Overall, + [结论]（更口语）
★★ We can see that + [结论]（偶尔用）
★★ It is evident that + [发现]（最强强调）

Details 段 · 万能句型大全

Details 段 = 选数字 + 写趋势 + 做对比。同一趋势至少用2-3种不同句型。

句型类型公式例句
1. S+V基本[对象] + [动词] + [副词] + [by数字] + [from年] [to年]The population of M-city increased dramatically by 2 million from 2000 to 2010.
2. saw句型[对象/时间/地点] + saw/witnessed + a/an + [adj] + [趋势N] + in + [对象]M-city saw a dramatic increase in population.
3. there beThere was + a/an + [adj] + [趋势N] + in + [对象] + [时间]There was a dramatic increase in the population of M-city.
4. 被动[趋势N] + could be seen/found + in + [对象] + [时间]A dramatic increase could be seen in the population.
5. 对比whileWhile [X] + [趋势], [Y] + [趋势]While X rose to 90 billion, Y fell to only 45 billion.
6. 排名followed by[X] was the most popular, followed by [Y] (at [%]) and [Z]Car use was the most popular, followed by cycling (at 15%) and walking.
Details段趋势词 速查
上升动词: rise· increase· grow· climb· jump· soar· rocket· surge
下降动词: decrease· fall· drop· decline· plunge· plummet
持平: remain stable· level off· reach a plateau
副词大幅: dramatically· sharply· significantly· considerably
副词小幅: slightly· modestly· marginally· gradually
名词搭配: a dramatic increase· a sharp decline· a steady growth

同一个趋势，三段话用三种不同句型写

趋势：手机通话分钟数从1995年的40亿上升到2002年的450亿。

句型1：The number of minutes of mobile calls rose dramatically from 4 billion in 1995 to 45 billion in 2002.

句型2：The period between 1995 and 2002 saw a dramatic increase in mobile call minutes, from 4 billion to 45 billion.

句型3：There was a dramatic increase in mobile phone usage, with the figure rising from 4 billion minutes in 1995 to 45 billion in 2002.
Task 2

大作文 · 四段万用框架 2-5-5-1

Universal Formula · 所有题型通用
2-5-5-1
Introduction = 2句 · Body 1 = 5句 · Body 2 = 5句 · Conclusion = 1句
Introduction: 2句 = 改写题目 + 表明立场
Body 1: ~5句 = Topic → Explain → Develop → Example → Conclude
Body 2: ~5句 = Topic → Explain → Develop → Example → Conclude
Conclusion: 1句 = 换词重申你的立场
总共约250-280词 · 不引新内容进入Conclusion

Body 段5句展开公式 — 所有题型通用

这段话是怎么一句一句往下推的逻辑：

句#功能怎么写
句1Topic 中心句这一段的核心论点。一句话说清楚这段论证什么
句2Explain 解释为什么你这样认为？解释中心句的逻辑
句3Develop 展开在什么情况下成立？泛例或进一步推理
句4Example 例子For example / For instance + 具体例子
句5Conclude 小结回扣中心句。这段论证了什么

示范：Space exploration 题 · Body 1

句1 Topic: Space programmes consume enormous financial resources.
句2 Explain: A single Mars mission can cost billions of dollars — money that could instead fund thousands of schools or hospitals.
句3 Develop: While space exploration yields some technological spin-offs, most citizens will never directly benefit from knowing more about distant planets.
句4 Example: For instance, the US space shuttle programme cost over $200 billion, yet produced few practical applications for everyday life.
句5 Conclude: It is difficult to justify such spending when basic public services remain underfunded.
Task 2

4种题型 · 各题型专属公式

题型1: Opinion 观点型

识别词: To what extent do you agree or disagree?

三种立场选一种：

  • 完全同意/不同意：I completely agree/disagree.
  • 部分同意：While I agree that..., I believe that...（最安全）
  • 平衡观点：不太推荐，容易显得没立场
万用Intro模板
It is sometimes argued that [改写题目观点]. While I agree that [对方有道理的部分], I believe that [你真正的立场].

Body结构：B1 = 先承认对方观点有一定道理 · B2 = 再转回自己立场（用Nevertheless / At the same time开头）

题型2: Discussion 讨论型

识别词: Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

固定结构：

Intro
People have different views about [题目话题]. While [观点A有道理], I believe that [观点B更好/你站哪边].

Body结构：

  • B1 = 讨论观点A（对方观点）— On the one hand...
  • B2 = 讨论观点B（你的立场）— On the other hand...
  • Conclusion = 重申你站哪边

注意：Body 1和Body 2不能论证同一个观点！必须是不同的view。

题型3: Problem & Solution

识别词: What are the problems? What measures can be taken?

固定结构：

  • B1 = 问题是什么 + 原因（3个问题或1-2个问题深度展开）
  • B2 = 解决方案（对应问题的解决措施）
万用Body模板
B1: There are several related problems that can be anticipated. The main issue is that [问题]. B2: There are several actions that governments/individuals could take to solve these problems. Firstly,...

注意：解决方案不能只是列出来——每个方案必须解释为什么有效。

题型4: Two-Part Question

两个独立问题,各写一段。同不同意类变体。

固定结构：

  • B1 = 回答第一个问题
  • B2 = 回答第二个问题
万用Intro模板
It is true that [背景]. There are several reasons why [Q1答案], and I believe that [Q2答案].

这种题型较少见。本质上就是Opinion题拆成两个问题。用Opinion的Body展开公式即可。

Task 2

Introduction + Conclusion · 万能公式

Introduction = 2句。绝不写背景介绍！

题型Intro公式
OpinionIt is sometimes argued that [改写题目]. I completely agree/disagree with this view.
Opinion（部分同意）It is sometimes argued that [改写题目]. While I agree that [对方部分], I believe that [我立场].
DiscussionPeople have different views about [题目话题]. While [观点A有道理], I believe that [我站观点B].
Problem/SolutionIt is true that [背景]. Although there will be negative consequences, societies can take steps to solve these problems.
改写的三个关键
1. 换动词：题目里的argue→有时说、认为；题目里的think→相信、主张
2. 换名词：people→individuals/the public；children→young people/teenagers
3. 换句式：Some people think that→It is sometimes argued that
不要照抄题目！一句话改写即可。

Conclusion = 1句。只做一件事：换词重述你的立场

题型Conclusion公式
OpinionIn conclusion, I believe that [换词重复你的立场].
DiscussionIn conclusion, while [承认A], I believe that [重申站B].
Problem/SolutionIn conclusion, [总结问题和方案方向].
Conclusion禁忌
✗ 不要引入新观点（新例子/新论证）
✗ 不要写"In conclusion, there are many ways to solve this problem"（太空泛）
✗ 不要照抄Introduction里的立场句——必须换词
✓ 用 I believe / it seems to me that / my own view is that 等换词
Task 1

串联记忆：一段 Details = 3-4 种句型拼出来

Task 1 的 Details 段本质上只有 4 种句型。同一段里混着用就不会单调

句型公式骨架Simon 真实例句什么时候用
1. S+V 主动 [对象] + [动词] + [adv] + [by数字] + [时间] The figure for mobile calls rose steadily to around 45 billion minutes by 2002. 某一条线/某一个数据的变化。最基础的句子，也是段落的"锚点"
2. saw / witnessed [对象/时间/地点] + saw + a/an + [adj] + [n.] + [时间] The year 2004 saw dramatic increases in the money spent on Fairtrade bananas in the UK. 接在 S+V 之后换主语——不重复用 the figure，用时间/地点做主语
3. there be / 被动 There was a [adj] + [n.] + in [对象]. / [n.] could be seen in [对象]. There was a sharp decline in the proportion of people using buses over the period. 需要变句型时使用。there be 最万能，任何趋势都能套。被动更正式
4. while 对比 / followed by 排名 While [A] [动词]+[数字], [B] [动词]+[数字]. / X was the most popular, followed by Y (at [%]). While 60% of water went to agriculture worldwide, a mere 8% was used by homes. 每段至少用一次对比。while 连两句最自然。followed by 用于排名
四种句型拼一段 Details（线图题示范）
趋势：手机通话分钟数从 1995 年 40 亿上升到 2002 年 450 亿，固定电话先升后降。

句1 (S+V 锚点)：The number of minutes of mobile calls increased dramatically from 4 billion in 1995 to 45 billion in 2002.
句2 (saw 变主语)：The same period saw a steady rise in national and international fixed-line calls.
句3 (there be 变句型)：However, there was a subsequent decline in local fixed-line calls after 1999.
句4 (while 对比收束)：While mobile usage surged, local landline calls fell from a peak of 90 billion to around 70 billion.

每段 Details 按图型选句型组合

题型句型组合策略
线图 / 有时间的柱图句1(S+V锚点) → 句2(saw换主语) → 句3(while对比或there be) → 句4(如果有第二条线再回S+V)
饼图 / 无时间的柱图句1(S+V占比) → 句2(followed by排名) → 句3(while对比) → 句4(被动或there be收尾)
表格句1(S+V锚点-选最大) → 句2(while对比最小) → 句3(saw/there be第二阶段数据) → 句4(followed by排第二的)

Task 2

串联记忆：一个 Body 段 = 5 种句型拼出来

Task 2 的 Body 段只有 5 种句型。一段 5 句话 = 5 种句型各用一次

句型公式骨架Simon 真实例句在段落中的位置
1. Topic 表态 While I accept that..., I believe that... / On the one hand, [中心句]. On the one hand, I accept that money is necessary in order for people to meet their basic needs. 句1 — 段首。说出这段论证什么
2. Cause 解释 This is because... / One reason why [X] is that... / This means that... This is because we all need money to pay for housing, food, bills, health care, and education. 句2 — 紧接中心句，解释为什么
3. Example 举例 For example, [具体例子]. / For instance, [具体案例]. / such as [并列例子]. For example, artists and musicians are known for choosing a career path that they love, but that does not always provide enough money. 句3-4 — 论证后用具体例子支撑
4. Contrast 对比/反证 By contrast / However / If [假设不成立], then [负面后果]. / Without [条件], [主语] would not... If people chose their jobs based on enjoyment alone, they might find it difficult to support themselves. 句4-5 — 例证后反向论证，强化论点
5. Conclude 回扣 In other words, [换说法回扣]. / It is difficult to [否定] when [事实]. In other words, an ageing population will mean a greater tax burden for working adults. 句5 — 段尾。一句话回扣本段中心论点
5种句型组装成一个完整 Body 段（Space Exploration 题 · Opinion型）
句1 Topic：On the one hand, I accept that space programmes consume enormous financial resources.
句2 Cause：This is because a single Mars mission can cost billions of dollars that could instead fund thousands of schools or hospitals.
句3 Example：For instance, the US space shuttle programme cost over $200 billion, yet produced few practical applications for everyday life.
句4 Contrast：If governments continue to pour money into space at the expense of public services, millions of citizens would suffer from underfunded healthcare and education.
句5 Conclude：In other words, it is difficult to justify such enormous spending when basic needs on Earth remain unmet.

最后检查 · 从6.5到7.0的关键10分钟

Task 1 检查项：

  • 时态是否统一？（有过去时间→全过去时）
  • Overview写了没有？（漏写=TR直接扣分）
  • Overview有没有写具体数字？（写了=错）
  • 同一趋势用了几个不同句型？（至少2个）
  • 动词有没有重复？（不要所有都用increased）

Task 2 检查项：
