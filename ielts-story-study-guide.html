Story A · 8 cards

Apteros Trading Mentor

Covers: A1 成功公司 · A2 成功商业 · A3 做计划 · A4 解决问题 · A5 乐于助人 · A6 名人 · G44 视频 · G57 建议
SMB Capital→ Apteros→ NADRO→ Mental Side→ Complete System→ Bilibili Sharing
Paragraph 1 · Core Philosophy I learned to listen to what the market is telling us, rather than trying to predict where it's going.
我学会了倾听市场在告诉我们什么，而不是试图预测市场会走向何方。
记忆钩子：listen vs predict 对立——这是整个 NADRO 方法论的根基。考官问任何关于这个人的问题，这句话就是你的开场。
Paragraph 2 · The Method His NADRO methodology — Narrative → Acceptance → DVA → Rhythm → Order Flow — gave me a structured way to read price action and order flow.
他的 NADRO 方法论——叙事→接受→DVA→节奏→订单流——给了我一个结构化的方式来解读价格行为和订单流。
NADRO Narrative Acceptance DVA Rhythm Order Flow structured way
记忆钩子：NADRO 五个字母 = 五个步骤。Narrative（故事）= 大背景，Acceptance（接受）= 心态，DVA（动态价值区）= 位置，Rhythm（节奏）= 时机，Order Flow（订单流）= 执行。按顺序念出来就像咒语。
Paragraph 3 · Mental Side He taught me the mental side of trading: truly accepting risk, recognizing how emotions cloud judgment, and understanding behavioral finance biases like loss aversion and anchoring. These are things no textbook could have taught me.
他教了我交易的心理层面：真正接受风险，认识到情绪如何蒙蔽判断，理解行为金融学偏差如损失厌恶和锚定效应。这些东西没有任何教科书能教会我。
mental side accepting risk emotions cloud judgment loss aversion anchoring no textbook
记忆钩子："No textbook could have taught me" 是金句。任何"人"类题目都可以用这句来收尾，强调这个人对你的不可替代性。
Paragraph 4 · Complete System As a pullback trader, he showed me that trading is a complete system — not scattered techniques. From narrative to order flow, every piece fits together.
作为一个回调交易员，他向我展示了交易是一个完整系统——而不是零散的技巧。从叙事到订单流，每一块都严丝合缝。
记忆钩子：pullback trader 是具体身份标签，比"trader"生动得多。"Every piece fits together" = 呼应前面的 NADRO 五个字母 = 闭环。
Paragraph 5 · Generosity What strikes me most is his willingness to share knowledge. His courses are available on Bilibili for anyone to learn from. For someone at his level, that generosity is rare and admirable.
最打动我的是他分享知识的意愿。他的课程在B 站上任何人都可以学习。对于他这个级别的人来说，这种慷慨是罕见且令人钦佩的。
记忆钩子：Bilibili 是具体平台名 = 真实可信。"Rare and admirable"是双形容词收尾，听起来更像英语母语者的表达习惯。
Story A · Keyword Chain
listen ≠ predict→ NADRO 5 steps→ mental side→ no textbook→ pullback trader→ complete system→ Bilibili→ rare & admirable
Story B · 4 cards

Grandmother's Garden & Legacy

Covers: B7 种菜 · B8 爱护自然 · B9 为家人骄傲 · G58 家庭重要的东西
Garden→ Philosophy→ Life Story→ Courage→ Legacy
Paragraph 1 · The Garden She loved growing vegetables. I still remember her small garden from my childhood, filled with all kinds of organic produce.
她热爱种植蔬菜。我仍然记得童年时她的小菜园，种满了各种有机作物。
记忆钩子：small garden + childhood = 画面感。old 记忆 + organic produce = 关键形容词对。
Paragraph 2 · Philosophy She never used chemical fertilizers or pesticides — she believed that natural was always best. She used to say, "The soil never lies. What you give, it gives back."
她从不用化肥和农药——她相信自然永远是最好的。她常说："土地从不骗人。你付出什么，它就回报什么。"
chemical fertilizers pesticides natural best soil never lies
记忆钩子："The soil never lies" 是核心引用——直接引语让考官觉得你在讲真事而非编造。任何"nature"类题目都可以复用这句话。
Paragraph 3 · Nature Lover She was a true lover of nature. For her, growing vegetables was not just a hobby — it was a philosophy of life: respect nature and live in harmony with it. She taught our generation what sustainable living meant, long before it became a buzzword.
她真正热爱自然。种菜不只是爱好——是人生哲学：尊重自然，与之和谐共处。早在可持续发展成为流行词之前，她就已经教会了我们这代人它的含义。
记忆钩子："Long before it became a buzzword" 是高分表达——展示了你对时代语境的把握。buzzword = 流行词/热词。
Paragraph 4 · Her Life She was a great mother and teacher. She raised three children on her own while working as a teacher. Through her own investments, she managed to leave a considerable amount of wealth for the family, ensuring a better life for the generations after her. Her foresight and discipline are things I deeply admire.
她是一位伟大的母亲和老师。独自抚养了三个孩子，同时从事教师工作。通过自己的投资，她为家庭留下了可观的财富，确保后代能有更好的生活。她的远见和自律让我深深敬佩。
记忆钩子：这里三个身份叠加——妈妈+教师+投资者——是"为家人骄傲"题目的核心。"Foresight and discipline"是两个抽象名词，展示词汇量。
Paragraph 5 · Final Days In her final days, she showed incredible courage. She fought her illness in the ICU until the very end, never giving up. Though she has left us, what she gave us — resilience, wisdom, a love for nature, and devotion to family — will never fade. I am so proud of her.
在她最后的日子里，她表现出难以置信的勇气。她在ICU与疾病战斗到底，从未放弃。虽然她已经离开了我们，但她留给我们的——韧性、智慧、对自然的热爱、对家庭的付出——永远不会褪色。我为她感到无比骄傲。
记忆钩子：结尾四个名词排列（resilience, wisdom, love for nature, devotion to family）= 四词总结一生。最后"I am so proud of her"简单有力，呼应题目。
Story B · Keyword Chain
small garden→ organic→ soil never lies→ philosophy of life→ sustainable living→ raised 3 children→ investments→ ICU courage→ resilience wisdom→ so proud
Story C · 4 cards

Childhood Friend & Doctor

Covers: C10 发小 · C11 语言学 · C12 医疗 · G55 朋友自学
Childhood→ Bond→ Languages→ Medicine
Paragraph 1 · Growing Up Together We grew up together. We've known each other since elementary school, and we were in the same class throughout junior high and senior high — both at the best schools in Tainan. We studied together, took exams together, and went through those formative years side by side. That kind of childhood bond is something you can never replicate with friends you meet later in life.
我们一起长大。从小学就认识，初中高中同班——都在台南最好的学校。一起学习、一起考试、一起度过成长岁月。这种童年纽带是你后来认识的朋友永远无法复制的。
grew up together formative years childhood bond never replicate
记忆钩子："Never replicate" 收尾有力。elementary → junior high → senior high → best schools in Tainan 四个节点串起成长轨迹。
Paragraph 2 · Language Talent His talent for languages is extraordinary. Fluent English is one thing, but he also taught himself Japanese and Spanish to a conversational level. When I asked him how, he said language isn't about rote memorization — it's about understanding the culture and mindset behind it. That reminds me of what Merit Black says about trading: understand the underlying logic first.
他的语言天赋惊人。流利英语不用说，他还自学了日语和西班牙语到会话水平。我问他怎么做到的，他说语言不是死记硬背——而是理解背后的文化和思维方式。这让我想起 Merit Black 谈交易的话：先理解底层逻辑。
记忆钩子：这里巧妙连接到交易（Merit Black引用）——展现跨领域思维能力。Japanese + Spanish 两种语言让例子更具体。"Rote memorization"是高分词汇。
Paragraph 3 · Doctor Path In Taiwan, studying medicine is reserved for only the very best students. He not only got in but became an excellent doctor. From the best high school in Tainan to medical school and then to practice — that path requires not just intelligence, but extraordinary self-discipline and perseverance. He is the hardest-working person I've ever known, and the friend I admire most.
在台湾，学医只有最顶尖的学生才能做到。他不仅考上了，还成了一名优秀的医生。从台南最好的高中到医学院再到临床——这条路不仅需要聪明，更需要非凡的自律和毅力。他是我认识最努力的人，也是我最敬佩的朋友。
记忆钩子：最后一句"hardest-working + admire most"双最级收尾——英语中最高级是用来强调的利器。整段从具体事实上升到品质评价。
Story C · Keyword Chain
grew up together→ best schools Tainan→ childhood bond→ never replicate→ Japanese Spanish→ not rote→ culture mindset→ medicine→ self-discipline→ hardest-working
Story D · 18 cards · Largest Story

Trading Journey: Struggle to Breakthrough

Covers: D13-D22 + F36-F43 + G53,G56,G59
University→ Fascinated→ Lost Money→ Holy Grail→ Mentor→ Small Class→ Breakthrough→ Passion
Paragraph 1 · How It Started It all started in university. I was studying financial engineering, surrounded by finance knowledge both in and out of class. A senior student I'd met through table tennis was doing quantitative trading on Bitcoin, and he showed me the gap between theory and the real market. I became fascinated. I thought: as a financial engineering student, shouldn't I understand this?
一切从大学开始。我学的是金融工程，课内课外都泡在金融知识里。一个通过乒乓球认识的学长在做比特币量化交易，他让我看到了理论与真实市场之间的鸿沟。我着迷了。我想：作为金融工程学生，我不应该搞懂这个吗？
记忆钩子：table tennis 细节让故事真实（不是模板答案）。"Gap between theory and the real market" 是你要反复使用的高频句子。
Paragraph 2 · The Struggle But the journey was far harder than I expected. The trading world is enormous — fundamental analysis, technical analysis, quantitative trading — and each branch has countless methods and theories. In this age of information overload, junk advice and financial scams are everywhere. I tried to learn every strategy and mastered none.
但这条路比我预想的难得多。交易世界极其庞大——基本面分析、技术分析、量化交易——每个分支都有无数的方法和理论。在信息过载的时代，垃圾建议和金融骗局无处不在。我试图学所有策略，结果一个都没掌握。
记忆钩子："Learn every strategy and mastered none" 是对仗结构，容易记。"Information overload" + "junk advice" + "scams" 三连击描述混乱期。
Paragraph 3 · Rock Bottom Emotional trading, revenge trading, entering without logic, chasing highs and lows, blind overconfidence — I made every mistake in the book. I lost quite a bit of money in the market, all while searching for a "holy grail" indicator that doesn't exist.
情绪交易、报复交易、无逻辑进场、追涨杀跌、盲目过度自信——我犯了书上所有的错误。在市场里亏了不少钱，同时一直在寻找一个根本不存在的"圣杯"指标。
emotional trading revenge trading chasing highs lows every mistake in the book holy grail doesn't exist
记忆钩子：五个交易错误的排比句——这是整篇最有力量的部分。一口气说出来展示你的 fluency。"Holy grail that doesn't exist" 是核心隐喻。
Paragraph 4 · Turning Point The turning point came when I found a mentor on YouTube. He had already achieved financial freedom through trading and wanted to give back — to help others, to contribute to society. He built a small class of fewer than ten people, carefully selecting students who had the time and determination to learn, and who promised to help others in turn once they succeeded. He guided me step by step in the right direction — not by giving me a magic formula, but by providing the right resources and framework so I could learn to think for myself.
转折点来了，我在YouTube 上找到一位导师。他已经通过交易实现了财务自由，想要回馈——帮助他人、贡献社会。他建立了一个不到十人的精选小班，挑选有时间和决心学习、并承诺成功后也帮助他人的学生。他一步一步引导我走向正确的方向——不是给我魔法公式，而是提供正确的资源和框架，让我学会自己思考。
记忆钩子："Not magic formula, but right resources and framework" 是对比结构。"Learn to think for myself" 是教育的终极目标——这句话考官会喜欢。
Paragraph 5 · The Breakthrough The real breakthrough didn't happen in a single moment. It came from countless days and nights of reviewing trades, thinking, losing money, and reflecting — and it finally crystallized after one major loss that wiped out my account. Instead of giving up, I pieced together everything I had learned over the years. From that point on, I focused purely on self-improvement and cut out all distractions.
真正的突破不是一瞬间发生的。它来自无数个日日夜夜的复盘、思考、亏损、反思——最终在一次爆仓的重大亏损后结晶成型。我没有放弃，而是把这些年学到的所有东西拼凑在一起。从那一刻起，我纯粹专注于自我提升，切掉了所有干扰。
记忆钩子："Countless days and nights" 强调时间投入。"Wiped out my account" 是交易者真实语言——比"lost money"生动。"Pieced together" 是拼图的意象。
Paragraph 6 · What Kept Me Going What kept me going wasn't talent — it was passion. Trading taught me not just a skill, but how to stay calm in the face of uncertainty, accept risk, and keep learning.
支撑我走下去的不是天赋——是热爱。交易教给我的不仅是一项技能，更是如何在不确定面前保持冷静、接受风险、持续学习。
记忆钩子："Not talent — passion" 对比简洁有力。结尾三个动词短语（stay calm / accept risk / keep learning）平行结构收尾，适合自然过渡到下一个话题。
Story D · Keyword Chain

Story E · 16 cards · Second Largest

Japan Road Trip: Kinosaki & Ine

Covers: E23-E35 + G48-G52,G54
Okayama→ Himeji→ Snow Stuck→ Kinosaki→ 7 Baths→ Oysters→ Ine Funaya
Paragraph 1 · Departure It was the first time in my life I bathed in a hot spring in the snow. Growing up in Taiwan, I had hardly ever seen snow. We set off from Okayama, rented a car with snow tires, and began a road trip that would cover nearly a thousand kilometers round trip.
那是我人生中第一次在雪中泡温泉。在台湾长大，我几乎没见过雪。我们从冈山出发，租了带雪胎的车，开始了一场来回近一千公里的公路旅行。
记忆钩子："First time in my life I bathed in a hot spring in the snow" 是完美的开场句——独特、画面感强。"Hardly ever seen snow" + "snow tires" + "thousand kilometers" 三要素快速建立场景。
Paragraph 2 · Kinosaki Atmosphere The town of Kinosaki Onsen looks like it was transported directly from the last century. The streets are lined with traditional Japanese architecture — wooden structures, low eaves, noren curtains hanging in doorways. Under the snow, it was like a scene from a film.
城崎温泉小镇看起来像是从上个世纪直接搬过来的。街道两旁是传统日式建筑——木结构、低屋檐、门口悬挂的暖帘。在雪中，像电影画面一样。
Kinosaki Onsen traditional architecture noren curtains scene from a film
Paragraph 3 · Seven Baths There are seven public hot springs, each with its own character: one is known as the "beauty bath," another is said to bring good fortune, and yet another has especially hot water. Visitors put on yukata robes and wooden sandals, and in sub-zero temperatures, hop from one bath to the next along snowy paths — a ritual called "sotoyu meguri."
有七个公共温泉，各有特色：一个叫"美人汤"，一个据说能带来好运，还有一个水温特别高。人们穿上浴衣和木屐，在零下的温度中沿着雪路从一个汤跳到另一个——这叫"外汤巡"。
记忆钩子："Sotoyu meguri" 日语词汇 = 增加文化真实性。"Yukata robes and wooden sandals" 视觉细节。"Sub-zero temperatures" 对比 hot spring = 强烈感官对比。
Paragraph 4 · Snow Rescue — The Most Unforgettable Moment The most unforgettable moment: as we neared Kinosaki, a heavy snowfall buried the entire road. Even with snow tires, our car got deeply stuck. The road was deserted — nothing but white in every direction. To our surprise, a Japanese grandfather, around sixty years old, came out without hesitation and started shoveling with us. We spent a full half hour in sub-zero temperatures, clearing the snow from under the car and the entire path ahead. Before leaving, we gave him the Taiwanese souvenirs we'd brought. We bowed deeply. We couldn't speak each other's language, but that moment of warmth was hotter than any hot spring.
最难忘的时刻：快到城崎时，一场大雪掩埋了整条路。即便有雪胎，车还是深深陷住了。路上空无一人——四周只有白色。令我们惊讶的是，一位六十岁左右的日本爷爷毫不犹豫地走出来，开始和我们一起铲雪。我们在零下温度中铲了整整半小时，清掉了车下和前方路上的所有雪。离开前，我们把带来的台湾伴手礼送给了他。我们深深鞠躬。语言不通，但那刻的温暖比任何温泉都热。
记忆钩子：这是整篇故事的高潮段。"Moment of warmth was hotter than any hot spring" 是双关——物理温暖 vs 人情温暖。"Deserted — nothing but white" 画面极简但有冲击力。
Paragraph 5 · Tiny Restaurant Another surprise was a tiny family-run restaurant with only five seats. Because we had traveled so far, almost no restaurants near Kinosaki were still open. We stumbled upon a family-run shop — a son and his elderly mother had turned the ground floor of their home into a restaurant. We ate the freshest fried oysters of our lives there. Using a translation app, we chatted happily with the son. In that tiny space, there was no tourist noise — just the simplest kindness between people.
另一个惊喜是一家只有五座的家庭小餐馆。因为旅途太远，城崎附近几乎没有餐馆还开着。我们偶遇一家店——儿子和老母亲把自家一楼变成了餐馆。我们吃到了人生中最新鲜的炸生蚝。用翻译软件，我们和儿子开心聊天。在那个小空间里，没有游客的嘈杂——只有人与人最简单的善意。
记忆钩子："Five seats" 具体数字 = 真实感。"Freshest fried oysters of our lives" 主观最高级。结尾 "No tourist noise — just the simplest kindness" 金句。
Paragraph 6 · Himeji & Ine We stopped along the way at Himeji Castle — a Japanese national treasure. Standing in front of it, you feel the weight of history. We also drove to Ine Funaya, Japan's only boat house village, where traditional wooden houses line the bay — boats parked on the ground floor, families living above. Mountains behind, sea ahead — like a living ukiyo-e painting. That place is almost unreal.
我们中途在姬路城停留——日本国宝。站在它面前，你能感受到历史的重量。我们还开车去了伊根舟屋，日本唯一的船屋村落，传统木屋沿湾排列——一楼停船，二楼住人。背山面海——像一幅活着的浮世绘。那个地方几乎不真实。
记忆钩子："Boats parked on the ground floor, families living above" 是伊根舟屋的标志描述。"Living ukiyo-e painting" 日本美学意象。"Weight of history" 简洁有力。
Paragraph 7 · Tatami & Reflection During our stay in Kinosaki, we slept on traditional tatami flooring — a large futon spread on the ground — which, honestly, I never quite got used to. But every night, lying there and thinking about the day's experiences, it all felt so surreal.
在城崎的日子里，我们睡在传统榻榻米上——大被褥铺在地上——说实话，我一直不太习惯。但每晚躺在那里回想当天的经历，一切都感觉如此梦幻。
记忆钩子："Never quite got used to" 诚实表达（不是所有体验都完美，这样更可信）。"So surreal" 简洁收尾，留白。

Story F · 9 cards

Trading Tech & Tools Life

Covers: F36-F44 · All tied to trading daily life
Slow Device→ Big Screen→ TradingView→ Costs→ Focus
Paragraph 1 · Slow Device My laptop and phone have been with me for years, and they've gotten painfully slow. Open one trading platform with a few charts, and the memory is almost full — the lag drives me crazy. For most people, a slow computer is just annoying, but in trading, a few seconds of delay can mean a completely different entry price.
我的笔记本和手机用了好几年，已经慢得令人痛苦。打开一个交易平台加载几张图，内存就快满了——卡顿让我抓狂。对大多数人来说，电脑慢只是烦人，但在交易中，几秒延迟 = 完全不同的进场价。
记忆钩子："Few seconds of delay = completely different entry price" 把日常抱怨上升到交易专业视角——这是 F 组的核心逻辑。
Paragraph 2 · Dream Setup I've been wanting a big screen setup — not just large, but something I can really spread out on. Trading requires watching multiple data streams at once: charts on different timeframes, order flow, news, market depth — one screen is never enough. I've even considered the Apple Vision Pro, imagining seven or eight virtual screens floating around me. It's not about looking cool — it's about efficiency. A good trading environment is part of the strategy itself.
我一直想要大屏幕设备——不仅要大，更要能铺开来用。交易需要同时看多个数据流：不同时间框架的图表、订单流、新闻、市场深度——一个屏幕永远不够。我甚至考虑过Apple Vision Pro，想象七八个虚拟屏幕漂浮在周围。不是为酷——是为效率。好的交易环境本身就是策略的一部分。
记忆钩子："Part of the strategy itself" 把硬件需求上升到策略高度。Vision Pro 是2026年的新鲜细节，考官会记住。
Paragraph 3 · TradingView My most-used app is TradingView. It has thousands of trading instruments — from crude oil to S&P E-mini to forex — plus all kinds of indicators and drawing tools. You can write your own strategy scripts or browse trading ideas shared by others. It's the app I open most every day, and it's been my most important tool for learning technical analysis. Unlike addictive social media, TradingView feels more like my digital workstation.
