Task 1 四段框架總覽

所有圖型通用。20分鐘內產出的四段結構。先理解全局，再背細節。

先看一個完整範例，建立整體感覺
Simon思考 · 拿到題目的第一反應

題目給我一張圖，我的大腦只做四件事：
1. 這圖在講什麼？→ 一句話改寫（Introduction）
2. 整體來看，最大的趨勢是什麼？→ 兩句話總結（Overview）
3. 選幾個關鍵數字，第一組 → 一段（Details 1）
4. 選另一組數字 → 另一段（Details 2）
沒了。就這四段。不要想別的。

【完整範例】UK telephone calls (Band 9, 197 words)

Introduction（1句）
The bar chart compares the amount of time spent by people in the UK on three different types of phone call between 1995 and 2002.

Overview（2句）
It is clear that calls made via local, fixed lines were the most popular type, in terms of overall usage, throughout the period shown. The lowest figures on the chart are for mobile calls, but this category also saw the most dramatic increase in user minutes.

Details 1（前半段：1995-1999）
In 1995, people in the UK used fixed lines for a total of just over 70 billion minutes for local calls, and about half of that amount for national or international calls. By contrast, mobile phones were only used for around 4 billion minutes. Over the following four years, the figures for all three types of phone call increased steadily.

Details 2（後半段：1999-2002）
By 1999, the amount of time spent on local calls using landlines had reached a peak at 90 billion minutes. Subsequently, the figure for this category fell, but the rise in the other two types of phone call continued. In 2002, the number of minutes of national / international landline calls passed 60 billion, while the figure for mobiles rose to around 45 billion minutes.

"You just need one sentence. Write it by paraphrasing the question statement."

1. Introduction 引入句

萬用公式：The + [圖型] + [動詞] + [主題說什麼] + [在哪裡] + [什麼時間]

★★★★★ 核心技能：改寫（Paraphrase）
Simon思考 · 改寫不是翻譯，是換詞

考官給你題目，你用不同的詞說同樣的意思。不需要改結構，只需要換詞。
三個必換：動詞換一個、時間表達換一個、名詞換一個（如果有的話）。
換完就停。多寫多錯。

題目原文（不要直接抄）
The chart below shows the amount of money spent by British parents on their children's sports between 2008 and 2014.
你的Introduction（改寫後）
The line graph compares the average monthly amount that parents in Britain spent on their children's sporting activities from 2008 to 2014.

觀察：shows→compares / below 刪掉 / money spent→average monthly amount / between...and...→from...to...

2. Overview 概覽段

全卷最重要的兩句話。考官先看這裡。不寫數字，只寫宏觀結論。

★★★★★ 寫Overview時Simon的思考過程
Simon思考 · 看著圖問自己三個問題

Q1：整體趨勢是什麼？ 上升？下降？先升後降？
Q2：誰最高？誰最低？ 找極值
Q3：有什麼特別值得注意的？ 交叉點？例外？某個時期變化特別大？

選兩個答案寫成兩句話。Q1+Q2 是最常見的組合。

★★★★★ 開頭句型（四選一，It is clear that 最常用）
★★★★★ It is clear that + [主要發現]
67篇中出現超過40次。你應該用這個。清晰、直接、沒有廢話。
★★★★ It is noticeable that + [明顯特徵]
強調「值得注意」。比 It is clear that 更正式一點點。
★★★ Overall, + [整體結論]
更口語化。放在段落第一句。使用頻率低於 It is clear that。
★★ We can see that + [可見結論]
可偶爾使用。不要每篇都用 We can see。
★★★★★ 三種兩句話組合策略（附 Simon 思考）

策略 A：最高 vs 最低（★★★★★ 最常用，佔60%以上）

  • 第一句：指出最高的類別 / 整體趨勢方向
  • 第二句：指出最低的 / 例外 / 最有戲的變化
It is clear that calls made via local, fixed lines were the most popular type, in terms of overall usage, throughout the period shown. The lowest figures on the chart are for mobile calls, but this category also saw the most dramatic increase in user minutes.
—— UK telephone calls
思路：句1=最高（local fixed）→ 句2=最低但增長最快（mobile）
It is noticeable that beef was by far the most popular of the four types of meat for the majority of the 57-year period. However, a considerable rise can be seen in the consumption of broilers, with figures eventually surpassing those for beef.
—— US meat consumption
思路：句1=最高（beef）→ 句2=例外反轉（broilers後來超過beef）

策略 B：一張圖一句（★★★★ 雙圖/混合圖必用）

  • 第一句：概括第一張圖的主要發現
  • 第二句：概括第二張圖的主要發現
It is clear that parents spent more money each year on their children's participation in sports over the 6-year period. In terms of the number of children taking part, football was significantly more popular than athletics and swimming.
—— Children's sports (雙線圖)
思路：句1=圖一（花費上升）→ 句2=圖二（足球最受歡迎）
Overall, females made up a higher proportion of people living alone than males, and this difference is particularly noticeable in the older age categories. We can also see that the most common number of bedrooms in a single-occupant home was two.
—— Living alone (bar+pie混合圖)
思路：句1=bar chart（女性多於男性）→ 句2=pie chart（兩臥最常見）

策略 C：整體趨勢 + 對比反轉（★★★★ 用於變化劇烈的圖）

  • 第一句：整體方向（上升/下降）
  • 第二句：一個明顯的例外或最顯著的變化
Both immigration and emigration rates rose over the period shown, but the figures for immigration were significantly higher. Net migration peaked in 2004 and 2007.
—— UK migration
思路：句1=兩者皆升但immigration更高 → 句2=淨遷移有兩個峰值

3. Details 細節段落

兩個Details段。每段3-5句。核心：分組→選數字→對比→趨勢。

★★★★★ Simon的分組思考（最重要！寫之前先想這個）
Simon思考 · 選數字之前，先決定「怎麼分兩段」

拿到圖，看完Overview後，你只有一個問題要回答：我要把這些數據切成哪兩組？

下面四種分法，選一種：

策略 1：按類別分（★★★★★ 最常用）

  • Details 1 = 正面/高的/A類  |  Details 2 = 負面/低的/B類
  • 適用：圓餅圖、滿意度調查、有明顯分類的柱狀圖
Why this way：Parkway Hotel 滿意度調查，有五個類別（excellent, good, satisfactory, poor, very poor）。Simon 把正面（excellent+good）放一段，負面（poor+very poor+satisfactory）放另一段。
你的思考：「正面評價 vs 負面評價，自然就是兩組。」
—— Essay 2: Parkway Hotel

策略 2：按時間段分（★★★★★ 有時間軸的圖必用）

  • Details 1 = 前半段時間  |  Details 2 = 後半段時間
  • 適用：線圖、有時間趨勢的柱狀圖
Why this way：UK telephone calls 從 1995 到 2002。Simon 以 1999 為界：前半（1995-1999）一段，後半（1999-2002）一段。因為1999是local calls的peak，是自然的轉折點。
你的思考：「找圖中的轉折點。以轉折點為界，分前後兩段。」
—— Essay 5: UK telephone calls

策略 3：按對象分（★★★★ 雙主體對比）

  • Details 1 = A組（boys / developed / 年輕人）  |  Details 2 = B組（girls / developing / 老年人）
  • 適用：按性別/年齡/國家分類的柱狀圖
Why this way：Children chatting vs gaming。數據按 boys 和 girls 分開。Simon 一段寫 boys，一段寫 girls。
你的思考：「圖表已經分好兩組（男/女），我照著分就行。」
—— Essay 4: Boys vs girls

策略 4：按圖分（★★★★ 混合圖只用這種）

  • Details 1 = 第一張圖的細節  |  Details 2 = 第二張圖的細節
  • 適用：bar+pie、graph+table 等混合題
Why this way：Living alone (bar + pie)。bar chart 寫一段，pie chart 寫另一段。混合圖只有這一種分法。
你的思考：「兩張圖，自然就是兩段。別想太多。」
—— Essay 6: Living alone
